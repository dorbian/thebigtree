TheBigTree Privacy Policy

Effective {EFFECTIVE_DATE} · Operator: {OPERATOR}

This Policy explains how TheBigTree processes personal data through its Discord application, games, web interfaces, and optional connected features. {OPERATOR} controls which optional features are enabled for this deployment.

1. Information processed

  • Discord identity and context: user IDs, server IDs, channel/thread/message IDs, display names, roles/permissions, and interaction metadata needed to route commands and enforce access.
  • Game and feature state: participation, Forest names, secret roles, actions, votes, judgements, last wills, scores, preferences, and related session state.
  • Message content when a feature requires it: Verdant’s immersive Forest-name relay receives the original message long enough to relay it under the game identity. Verdant does not intentionally create a second persistent transcript of ordinary relayed Living Circle or Lost Forest conversation.
  • Language Services: when enabled, prompts, generated replies, and bounded recent conversation memory may be stored in PostgreSQL. Optional Discord knowledge retrieval fetches relevant messages on demand from channels the bot is already permitted to read rather than maintaining a full Discord-history mirror.
  • Uploads: media submitted to features that accept uploads, including generated thumbnail/preview derivatives.
  • Security and administration data: authentication principals/tokens, permission assignments, request/audit metadata, IP information where needed for security logs, and operational diagnostics.

2. Purposes and legal bases

Information is processed to deliver requested commands and games, maintain and recover session state, enforce permissions, relay player messages, provide optional language features, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, secure the service, and comply with legal obligations. Where GDPR or similar law applies, processing may rely on performance of the requested service, legitimate interests in operating and securing it, consent where appropriate, and legal obligations.

3. Third parties

TheBigTree exchanges data with Discord as needed for bot interactions. If Language Services are enabled, relevant prompts/context may be sent to the configured provider, such as OpenAI or MiniMax. Optional integrations contact their named provider only when that feature is enabled. TheBigTree does not sell personal data and does not build advertising profiles of players.

4. Retention

Retention depends on the feature and deployment configuration. Active game/session state remains while needed to run or recover a session and may remain in service history until maintenance or deletion. Language memory is bounded by configured age and row limits. Uploads remain until deleted by the relevant workflow or administrator. Operational logs follow the deployment’s logging and backup policy; backups may temporarily retain deleted records until rotation completes.

5. Security

TheBigTree separates public and administrative surfaces, applies capability checks to protected actions, keeps configured provider secrets server-side, and limits Discord access to permissions granted to the bot. No system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and rights

You may stop interacting with TheBigTree or leave a game where its rules allow it. Server administrators can remove the application. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing of, or receive a portable copy of personal data associated with you. Identity verification may be required before fulfilling a request.

7. Children and international processing

TheBigTree is not intended for anyone below Discord’s minimum permitted age or a higher local minimum. Discord, hosting providers, or optional AI/integration providers may process data in other countries under their own privacy terms and transfer mechanisms.
